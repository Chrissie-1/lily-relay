# Lily Relay — Positioning

## One-line description

Lily Relay is the workflow-aware coordination layer that turns customer promises into aligned execution and reusable proof across Sales, FDE, and Product.

## Category

Cross-functional operations orchestration.

Lily Relay is not a CRM, project tracker, call recorder, or analytics dashboard. Those tools record local activity. Relay coordinates the decisions and evidence that must move between them.

## The problem

Customer intent starts in a conversation, commercial commitments land in a CRM, feasibility decisions happen with FDE, delivery changes live in engineering tools, and outcome evidence appears in product systems. At every handoff, context is translated, delayed, or lost.

The result is handoff debt: unsupported promises, rework, unclear ownership, missing baselines, scope surprises, and outcomes that cannot be credibly reused.

## Unique value proposition

**Configure the coordination, not the company.**

Lily Relay first learns how an organization already operates—its systems, terminology, owners, approval boundaries, and evidence sources. It then creates one shared Outcome Contract that routes the right decision to the right team, preserves the source context, reconciles promise with reality, and returns approved truth to the tools where people already work.

## Why it is different

1. **Workflow-aware:** adapts to the company's real operating model instead of imposing a generic process.
2. **Decision-centered:** moves accountable decisions across teams rather than creating another passive dashboard.
3. **Evidence-native:** defines success before delivery and keeps every reusable outcome tied to its source and measurement window.
4. **System-friendly:** works through existing CRMs, conversation tools, delivery trackers, product telemetry, and collaboration channels.
5. **Learning loop:** turns scope drift, approval patterns, and proof gaps into reusable operating knowledge for future opportunities.

## Primary audiences

- Revenue and Sales Operations leaders reducing promise-to-delivery friction
- FDE and Solutions leaders managing feasibility, dependencies, and scope integrity
- Product leaders connecting customer commitments to measurable outcomes
- Executive operators responsible for cross-functional execution quality

## Demonstration promise

In one synthetic workflow, a viewer can map the operating stack, extract customer commitments, validate scope, define success, reconcile what shipped, govern outcome language, and release approved proof back to Sales.

## Short pitch

Your CRM knows the deal. Your delivery system knows the work. Your product stack knows what happened. Lily Relay knows how those truths must move between teams. It gives Sales, FDE, and Product one workflow-aware contract from promise to proven outcome—without replacing the tools they already use.
